Rules & Guide

🎯 Objective

Race all 4 of your tokens around the board and into your center Home Triangle before your opponents.

🎲 Leaving Home Base

You must roll a 6 to bring a token out of your starting yard onto your start cell.

⚡ Extra Turns

Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent token, or getting a token safely into the home goal awards an immediate Extra Turn!

⚔️ Capturing & Safe Cells

Landing on an opponent's token sends it back to their yard! Tokens cannot be captured on Safe Star (⭐) cells or on starting squares.

👑 Exact Roll to Finish

Tokens require the exact number of steps to reach the final center crown cell.
